### 섹션 2 - Mission
```
Our mission is to provide valuable, educational content to Korean seniors aged 50 to 60. We cover topics like health and wellness, financial literacy, and lifestyle tips. Every piece of content we create is designed to inform, educate, and add genuine value to our viewers' lives.

### 섹션 3 - Process ⭐
```
Let me explain our content creation workflow. Our process involves multiple stages, and each stage requires human review and approval before moving forward. First, our content strategist selects topics based on audience research and trending interests. Then, our writers develop scripts with the help of AI tools. However, every script is manually reviewed and edited by our editorial team. Next, our design team creates visuals. Again, each image and video clip is reviewed for quality and brand consistency. Finally, before any video is published, our quality assurance team performs a thorough review to ensure compliance with YouTube's Community Guidelines. Only after passing all these checkpoints, the content is approved for publication.
```

### 섹션 4 - Upload
```
Once our team approves a video, we use the YouTube Data API to upload it efficiently. This is where we need the API quota. We use the videos.insert endpoint for uploading, and videos.update for setting proper titles, descriptions, and tags. The API allows us to maintain a consistent publishing schedule while ensuring every upload has been properly reviewed by our team.
```

```
We believe our content contributes positively to the YouTube ecosystem. Our videos are original, educational, and designed to keep viewers engaged. We focus on watch time and viewer satisfaction, which aligns perfectly with YouTube's algorithm and recommendation system. By providing quality content consistently, we help YouTube deliver valuable videos to an underserved demographic: Korean seniors who are looking for trustworthy information. This is a win-win for YouTube, for our viewers, and for our team.
